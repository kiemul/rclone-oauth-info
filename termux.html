Personal Termux rclone OAuth information

This page explains a privately used Google OAuth client in the gogcli application configuration, used for rclone on the account owner's Android Termux environment.

Unlike the separate metadata-only Windows audit client, this rclone client can request full Google Drive access, including listing, downloading, uploading, modifying, moving and deleting files, to perform operations explicitly requested by its owner.

The account owner operates the software on devices under their control. This is not a public hosted storage service. This website contains only OAuth disclosure information, not the user's files or credentials.

Read the privacy policy to understand how the connection uses Google account data.
