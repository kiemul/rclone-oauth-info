Personal Stremio Google Drive addon

PrivacyTerms

This personal, self-hosted addon lets its owner find and play their own Google Drive videos through Stremio. It looks for matching movies and TV episodes in the owner's configured library folders.

The addon requests read-only Google Drive access so it can list file metadata and stream chosen videos. Google grants that scope across the account; the addon applies its own folder restrictions when serving requests.

The addon runs on Cloudflare Workers. Its private addon URL is a bearer credential and should not be shared. This page contains no credentials and does not receive Google Drive files.
