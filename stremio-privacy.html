Privacy

Effective 30 October 2026

Access and use

With the owner's permission, the addon uses the Google Drive read-only scope to list file and folder metadata and read selected video content. The Google permission applies to files accessible by the account; the addon restricts its responses to configured library folders.

Processing and storage

The addon runs on Cloudflare Workers. Google OAuth credentials are stored as Cloudflare Worker secrets and in the owner's Proton Pass vault. Selected video content passes through Cloudflare to the Stremio player. Stremio receives addon and stream URLs and metadata needed to show and play results. Operational logs may contain request or file metadata. The static website does not receive Google Drive content or credentials.

Sharing and access

Anyone with the private addon URL may use it to access videos exposed by the addon. The owner should keep it private and rotate it if exposed. No advertising use or sale of Google user data is intended.

Revocation

The owner can remove access through Google Account connections, remove the Stremio addon, and delete the relevant Cloudflare secrets and Proton Pass entries. Cloudflare and Stremio may retain operational data according to their own services' settings and policies.
